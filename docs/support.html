How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by your store, not inside the app. On iPhone: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Aloud. On Android: open Google Play → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Aloud. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I paid, but the app doesn't show Pro

Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If that doesn't help, email us.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, through Google Play's order history.

My speech doesn't show up as text

The app needs microphone access, and on iPhone also speech recognition. Check both in your phone's settings under Aloud. Recognition is in English for now.

How do I change my name or country?

In the app: Settings → Edit profile.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile, rounds and transcripts immediately and cannot be undone. It does not cancel a subscription, so cancel that first. See the Privacy Policy for details.
